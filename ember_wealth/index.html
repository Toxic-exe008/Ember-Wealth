About Me

Financial clarity starts with understanding your goals — not the market.

I'm Arjun Mehta, an independent SEBI-registered investment advisor and wealth strategist based in Bengaluru. After over a decade at two of India's leading asset management firms, I founded Meridian Wealth Advisory to offer something the industry rarely provides: unbiased, client-first financial guidance.

My practice is intentionally small. I work with a limited number of clients each year — professionals, business owners, and multi-generational families — because I believe genuinely thoughtful advice requires time, depth, and a relationship built on trust. Every engagement begins with listening, not selling.

Let's Have a Conversation

Fiduciary Always

As a SEBI-registered advisor, I am legally required to act in your best interest — not earn commissions from products I recommend. Your interests come first, always.

Truly Personalised

No cookie-cutter plans. Every strategy is built from scratch around your income structure, risk appetite, life goals, and family dynamics. This is advice made for you.

Radical Transparency

I explain everything in plain language — no jargon, no pressure. You will always know exactly what you own, why you own it, and what it costs.
